About

I am a Post-doc at the Academy of Mathematics and Systems Science, Chinese Academy of Science, advised by Prof. Chenjie Fan.

In 2017, I obtained my BSc degree in Beijing Normal University. After that, I obtained my PhD at Fudan University, 2017-2021, under the supervision of Prof. Bobo Hua.

My current research focuses on dispersive equations, from both deterministic and probablistic sides.
